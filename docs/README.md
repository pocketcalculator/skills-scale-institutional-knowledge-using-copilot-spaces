# OctoAcme Project Management Process Documentation

Welcome to OctoAcme's project management process documentation! This folder serves as the central hub for understanding how we plan, execute, and deliver projects across the organization. Whether you're a new team member looking to get up to speed or an experienced contributor seeking guidance on specific processes, you'll find everything you need here.

## Overview of OctoAcme's Project Management Approach

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Our methodology spans the complete project lifecycle—from initial concept validation through deployment and continuous improvement—ensuring that every project delivers measurable customer value while maintaining high quality standards.

At the core of our approach are three key roles working in close collaboration: Project Managers who coordinate delivery, schedules, and risk mitigation; Product Managers who define outcomes, prioritize work, and measure success; and cross-functional delivery teams comprising developers, QA specialists, and stakeholders. Each project follows a structured lifecycle beginning with initiation (validating business need and aligning stakeholders), moving through planning (creating actionable backlogs and release plans), execution (building and testing with continuous feedback), release (deploying with appropriate safeguards), and closing with retrospectives that capture learnings for future improvements.

Our communication strategy is built around transparency and regular cadence: weekly syncs between project and product leads, twice-weekly team standups, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations when needed. We maintain a clear escalation path from team-level triage through PM coordination to executive sponsorship for business-critical issues. All projects use standardized artifacts including project charters, risk registers, acceptance criteria, and retrospective action items, which are tracked in our project boards and repositories.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout our process, not treated as a final gate. We emphasize small, testable increments with comprehensive test coverage (unit, integration, and end-to-end), automated CI/CD pipelines with security scanning, code review requirements, and clearly defined Definitions of Done. Every release includes pre-deployment checks, smoke testing, rollback plans, and post-deployment verification. This integrated approach to quality, combined with our emphasis on iterative delivery and continuous improvement, enables us to ship reliably while maintaining the flexibility to respond to changing requirements and learnings.

## Getting Started

If you're new to OctoAcme's project management practices, we recommend:

1. Start with the **Project Management Overview** to understand our core principles and approach
2. Review **Roles and Personas** to understand your role and how you'll interact with others
3. Follow the lifecycle documents in order (Initiation → Planning → Execution → Release → Retrospective) as you work through your first project
4. Keep the **Risk Management and Communication** guide handy for ongoing reference
